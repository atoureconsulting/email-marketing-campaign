### Psychographics
| Field | Value |

| **Current Situation** | Working a 9-5 they don't love. Watched a lot of HGTV and YouTube but intimidated by the financial risk. May have tried wholesaling but found it too competitive. |
| **Biggest Fear** | Losing money on a bad deal and being stuck with a property they can't sell. Looking stupid in front of family who told them "just keep your job." |
| **Buying Trigger** | Seeing Alex's exact numbers on a real deal (purchase price, rehab cost, sale price, profit) and realizing the deals are smaller and less risky than they imagined. |

### Top Pains (ranked by intensity)
1. "I don't have $100K sitting around to flip a house. Every guru makes it sound like you need a fortune to start."
2. "I'm terrified of buying a money pit. What if the inspection misses something and I'm underwater?"
3. "I don't know any contractors, lenders, or agents. I feel like I'm trying to enter a world where everyone already knows each other."

### Top Desires (ranked by pull)
1. Quit their 9-5 within 2 years by building a flipping business that replaces their income
2. Complete one successful flip to prove to themselves (and their family) that this is real
3. Have a repeatable system so they're not guessing on every deal

### Top Objections (ranked by frequency)
1. "I can't afford $3,500 for a coaching program when I don't even have money to flip yet." — Reframe: The program teaches you to use hard money lenders and the Micro Flip method. You don't need personal capital. The $3,500 pays for itself on your first deal.
2. "Can't I just learn this on YouTube for free?" — Reframe: You can learn what a flip is on YouTube. You can't get someone to review your actual deals, connect you with vetted lenders, or walk you through your first closing.
3. "What if the market crashes?" — Reframe: Micro Flips are cosmetic-only, under $30K total. Your exposure is tiny compared to traditional flips. Even in a down market, move-in ready homes sell.

## Voice & Brand Profile

### Voice Dimensions
| Dimension | Scale | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| **Casual vs. Formal** | 3 (casual but not slangy) | Conversational, approachable, like explaining to a friend over coffee |
| **Aggressive vs. Supportive** | 6 (leans supportive) | Encouraging but calls out excuses when needed. More coach than drill sergeant. |
| **Humor vs. Serious** | 5 (balanced) | Uses self-deprecating humor about early mistakes, but gets serious about numbers |
| **Short vs. Long Sentences** | 5 (mixed) | Alternates between punchy one-liners and explanatory sentences |
| **Simple vs. Complex Vocabulary** | 3 (simple) | Avoids jargon, explains real estate terms when used |

### Brand Language
- **Words/phrases to USE:** "here's what that actually looks like," "let me show you the numbers," "micro flip," "cosmetic flip," "you don't need to be rich to start," "run the numbers," "let's break this down," "the deal doesn't care about your feelings"
- **Words/phrases to NEVER USE:** "passive income" (Alex hates this term — flipping is active), "get rich quick," "easy money," "hustle," "grind," any corporate jargon
- **Profanity:** Very rarely. Maybe "damn" once per email for emphasis. Never f-bombs in written content.
- **Faith/values integration:** Not part of the brand voice. Alex keeps it secular and numbers-focused.

### Voice Examples

```
I almost didn't do my first flip. I spent four months analyzing deals on Zillow, running numbers in a spreadsheet I built at 2am, and convincing myself I needed "just a little more research." You know what finally got me to move? My buddy closed on a duplex that I had looked at first. Same deal. Same numbers I ran. He made $22K. I made another spreadsheet. That was the last time I let analysis paralysis win.
```

```
People hear "house flip" and picture a $400K gut renovation with a crew of 15. That's TV. A Micro Flip is a $180K house that needs paint, flooring, landscaping, and maybe a kitchen refresh. Total rehab: $18K. Timeline: 6 weeks. Profit: $25-40K. You don't need to be a contractor. You need a checklist and a phone.
```

### Tone Shifts by Context
| Context | Energy | Example |
|---------|--------|---------|
| **Motivational** | Warm, encouraging, story-driven | "My first flip made $19K. My second lost $3K. My third made $41K. The path isn't straight. It doesn't have to be." |
| **Educational** | Patient, specific, numbers-heavy | "Here's the formula: ARV minus rehab minus purchase minus holding costs minus selling costs = your profit. Let me walk through each one." |
| **Vulnerable** | Honest about failures, no sugarcoating | "I lost money on my second flip because I trusted a contractor I found on Craigslist. That mistake cost me $3,200 and three months." |
| **Sales** | Confident, proof-driven, low-pressure | "The Accelerator isn't for everyone. If you're looking for a get-rich-quick scheme, this isn't it. But if you want someone to walk you through your first deal step by step, that's what this is." |
