# AToure Consulting — Agency Profile

## Who we are
AToure Consulting is a talent management and brand consultancy specialising in African and diaspora talent. We connect brands, media, events, and businesses with a curated roster of influencers, creatives, artists, and cultural figures.

## What we offer brands
- **Talent partnerships**: Matching brands with the right creator or talent for campaigns, events, content production, and long-term ambassador roles
- **Cultural reach**: Our talent are embedded in African, Afro-Caribbean, and diaspora communities — audiences that are often hard to reach authentically through mainstream channels
- **Content creation**: High-quality content across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube — short-form, editorial, documentary, lifestyle
- **Event appearances**: Talent available for launches, openings, panels, performances, hosting, and brand activations
- **Creative direction**: Several of our talent also operate as photographers, stylists, directors, and creative consultants
- **Tour & travel**: We work with touring artists and talent who regularly move between London, Paris, Abidjan, Accra, and New York — creating natural partnership opportunities for travel, hospitality, and lifestyle brands

## Our talent roster covers
- Fashion (models, stylists, content creators)
- Lifestyle and luxury
- Music and entertainment (artists, DJs, performers, presenters)
- Beauty (MUAs, skincare, haircare creators)
- Sport and fitness
- Food and hospitality content creators
- Travel and culture journalists and influencers
- Afro-diasporic cultural voices and thought leaders

## Geographic footprint
Our talent are based in and regularly travel between:
- London and the UK
- Paris and francophone Europe
- Abidjan, Dakar, Accra and major West African cities
- New York and the US diaspora market

Many of our talent tour, attend fashion weeks, shoot internationally, and attend cultural events globally.

## How collaborations work
We are flexible: one-off campaigns, retainer ambassador deals, event appearances, content packages, co-branded activations. We adapt to what makes commercial sense for the brand.

## What makes us different
We do not do volume. We curate. Every talent on our roster has authentic audiences, real creative credibility, and the professionalism that brand partnerships require.
